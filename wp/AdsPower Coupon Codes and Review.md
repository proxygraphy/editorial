Profiles can't see or affect each other, even when they're open side by side. AdsPower **replaces** your fingerprint with realistic values. It does not just block fingerprinting scripts, which is important because blocking is itself a warning sign to anti-fraud systems.

Fingerprint settings you can control:

## 🌍 Proxy Integration in AdsPower

AdsPower gives each profile a unique **fingerprint**. A proxy gives each profile a unique **IP address**. You need both: if two profiles share an IP, platforms can still link them.

AdsPower supports:

- 🏠 **Residential proxies**: best for social media and ad accounts
- 📶 **Mobile (4G/5G) proxies**: best for TikTok, Instagram and strict platforms
- 🏢 **Static ISP proxies**: best for long-term accounts that need the same IP every time
- 🖥️ **Datacenter proxies**: cheap and fast, for less strict sites

Supported protocols: **HTTP, HTTPS and SOCKS5**. We recommend **SOCKS5** where possible because it also sends DNS requests through the proxy, which prevents DNS leaks.

### ⚙️ Quick Proxy Setup

1. Click **New Profile** on the dashboard.
2. Scroll to **Proxy** and select **SOCKS5** (or HTTP).
3. Enter **host, port, username and password** from your proxy provider.
4. Click **Check Proxy** and confirm the IP, country and status.
5. Click **OK / Save**, then **Open** the profile.

**Golden rule:** use **one unique IP per profile**. Shared IPs are the most common reason accounts get banned together.
